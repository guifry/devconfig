# Word Forward, Word Out

Explain the thing one line at a time.

## The rules

- One short phrase per line.
- Each line follows from the line before it.
- Plain simple English. Grade school level.
- No paragraphs. No bullet nesting.
- No jargon. If a term is needed, define it on the next line.
- Use real numbers, not vague ones.
- Say the thing. Don't hedge, don't caveat first.

## The shape

Start where the reader already is.
Walk forward one step at a time.
Stop when the idea is finished.

## Example

Everyone gets £12,570 of tax-free income.
That's called your allowance.
It means the first £12,570 escapes tax.
Now earn more than £100,000.
The tax office starts taking that allowance back.
You lose £1 of allowance for every £2 you earn.
So you keep 38p of every extra pound.

## Rules of thumb

Show the numbers.
Then show what they mean.
Then give the rule to follow.
